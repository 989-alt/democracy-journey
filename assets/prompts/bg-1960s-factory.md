# 1. Scene
Inside a late-1960s Korean garment and wig factory: long rows of sewing machines under a low ceiling, young women workers in headscarves bent over their work, bare light bulbs on cords, bolts of cloth and stacks of boxes against the wall, a small clock on the wall with a blank face. Tired but orderly, not frightening. The lower third of the canvas is clean, uncluttered ground kept empty for a dialogue panel and choice cards, and the top edge is a plain strip for a chapter label. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level wide shot down the aisle between rows of machines.
# 3. Lighting
Harsh bare-bulb light from above with warm reflections off the cloth, dim corners.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
