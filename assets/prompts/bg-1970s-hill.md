# 1. Scene
A steep hillside neighbourhood of small tin-roofed and tar-paper houses in 1970s Seoul, narrow concrete stairs climbing between them, laundry lines, a water jar, children playing on a landing; in the distance across the valley a brand-new row of apartment blocks under construction with a crane. The lower third of the canvas is clean, uncluttered ground kept empty for a dialogue panel and choice cards, and the top edge is a plain strip for a chapter label. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Elevated wide shot from halfway up the hill looking across to the distant construction.
# 3. Lighting
Late afternoon sun from the right, long warm shadows down the stairs.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
