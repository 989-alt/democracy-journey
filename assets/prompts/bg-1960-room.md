# 1. Scene
Inside a small Korean house in the spring of 1960: a modest ondol room with papered walls and a low wooden table, a vacuum-tube radio on a chest glowing faintly, a lattice window on the right letting in afternoon light, a folded newspaper and a wall calendar as plain paper surfaces. A ten-year-old Korean girl with a bob haircut in a plain cotton blouse and dark skirt sits at the left of the room listening intently, her father in a white shirt and vest beside the radio and her mother in a hanbok-style blouse pouring barley tea. The lower third of the canvas is clean warm floor kept empty for a dialogue panel and choice cards, and the upper left corner is a plain wall for a chapter label. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster and sign is a plain blank paper surface.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level wide shot of the room from the doorway, characters at mid distance and crisp, background falls off softly.
# 3. Lighting
Afternoon window light from the right, soft warm key with cool shadow fill from the papered wall, faint glow from the radio dial.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
