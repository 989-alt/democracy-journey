# 1. Scene
An empty narrow alley in a 1960 Korean town at dawn: low tiled roofs on both sides, a leaning wooden telegraph pole, a water pump, a cat on a wall, and the far end of the alley opening onto bright morning light with a cherry tree in bloom. The alley is empty of people. The composition leaves the right third brighter and more colourful than the rest. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level view straight down the alley, vanishing point slightly right of centre.
# 3. Lighting
Cool blue dawn on the left warming to golden morning light at the far right end.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
