# 1. Scene
One wide illustration divided exactly down the middle by a single vertical sepia ink line into two equal panels. The left panel is painted in faded sepia monochrome and the right panel in full warm colour. THEN: a 1960 village polling station where three men walk together in a row to one open voting table and an official hands out a pre-folded ballot. TODAY: a bright modern Korean polling station with a private cardboard voting booth, an official checking an identity card at a desk, a sealed transparent ballot box, and a citizen stamping a blank ballot in privacy. Each panel is a complete small scene with its own ground and sky, nothing crossing the centre line. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
