# 1. Scene
Inside a 1960 Korean village polling station set up in a school classroom: at the centre a long wooden table with a wooden ballot box and two officials seated behind it; on the left three men in worn jackets walking together in a tight row toward one open voting table; near the left door a man slipping a small paper envelope into another man's hand; on the right an official handing an elderly man a folded ballot that is already creased; at the far right a curtained voting booth with its curtain drawn; at the front right a nurse pushing a patient in a wheelchair holding a blank ballot. Six distinct groups, each clearly separated with space around it. The lower third of the canvas is clean, uncluttered ground kept empty for a dialogue panel and choice cards, and the top edge is a plain strip for a chapter label. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Slightly elevated wide shot from the back of the room so every group is visible and none overlaps.
# 3. Lighting
Flat overcast daylight from tall classroom windows, soft shadows.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
