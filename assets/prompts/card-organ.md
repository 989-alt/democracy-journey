# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of twelve small painted symbols arranged in a grid of four columns and three rows with equal cells and generous even spacing, each centred in its cell, on a flat solid magenta background. Row one: a rolled paper scroll with a quill, a brass magnifying glass over an open ledger, a wooden abacus beside a small stack of coins, a wooden podium with a microphone. Row two: a round table with chairs seen from above, a row of three plain government office buildings with blank signboards, a pair of brass balance scales, three ascending stone steps with a small gavel on the top step. Row three: a magnifying glass pointed at a small office building, a red wax seal on a document with a diagonal ink stroke across it, a torn document with a gavel resting on it, a wooden ballot box. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on orthographic view, every symbol the same visual weight and size, aligned to the grid.
# 3. Lighting
Soft window light from the upper left, faint contact shadow under each symbol.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, ink #2B2B2B on flat magenta #FF00FF. Around 3800K, gently muted.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor with ink outline, fully opaque paint, crisp silhouettes against the flat magenta.
