# 1. Scene
The newly opened 1970 expressway cutting through green Korean hills: a fresh concrete highway with painted lanes, a round-nosed intercity bus and a cargo truck driving away toward the horizon, a small toll gate with a blank sign, rice fields on either side. The lower third of the canvas is clean, uncluttered ground kept empty for a dialogue panel and choice cards, and the top edge is a plain strip for a chapter label. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Elevated three-quarter view from an overpass looking down the road toward the hills.
# 3. Lighting
Clear morning light from the left, cool haze over the far hills.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
