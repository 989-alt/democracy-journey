# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of three bust portraits of the same Korean woman in her seventies today: white hair in a low bun with the same small hairpin, thin glasses, a soft cardigan, arranged in a row of three equal cells with generous even spacing on a flat solid magenta background: left cell calm and attentive with a slight smile, middle cell joyful with a bright open smile, right cell troubled with a worried frown and downcast eyes. The same face, hair and clothing in all three, facing the viewer slightly turned. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on portraits, head and shoulders filling about seventy percent of each cell, all three the same size and eye level.
# 3. Lighting
Soft window light from the upper left, gentle fill, light rim on the hair.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, ink #2B2B2B on flat magenta #FF00FF. Around 3800K, gently muted.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper with ink line, fully opaque paint, crisp silhouettes against the flat magenta.
