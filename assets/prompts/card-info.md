# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of four painted objects arranged in a grid of two columns and two rows with equal cells and generous even spacing, each centred in its cell, on a flat solid magenta background. Top left: a 1980 vacuum-tube tabletop radio with a softly glowing dial. Top right: an opened handwritten-style letter with blank pages beside its envelope with a stamp. Bottom left: a glossy foreign magazine page with a blank photo frame and a press camera resting on it. Bottom right: a market basket of vegetables with two blank paper speech-bubble notes floating above it. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on orthographic view, every object the same visual weight, aligned to the grid.
# 3. Lighting
Soft window light from the upper left, faint contact shadow under each object.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, ink #2B2B2B on flat magenta #FF00FF. Around 3800K, gently muted.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor with ink outline, fully opaque paint, crisp silhouettes against the flat magenta.
