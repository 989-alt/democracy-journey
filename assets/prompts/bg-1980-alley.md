# 1. Scene
A narrow city alley in Gwangju in May 1980 at dusk: a small shop with its metal shutter pulled down, neighbours of different ages gathered around a tabletop radio set on a wooden crate, an older woman holding a child's hand, a young man leaning in to listen, worried quiet faces, a dim streetlamp beginning to glow, a folded blank newspaper on the step. Only neighbours on foot are present. The lower third of the canvas is clean, uncluttered ground kept empty for a dialogue panel and choice cards, and the top edge is a plain strip for a chapter label. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide shot from a few steps back, the group slightly left of centre.
# 3. Lighting
Blue dusk with one warm pool of streetlamp light over the radio.
# 4. Color grading
Warm memoir palette: paper cream #F2E8DC, sepia brown #8C7355, apricot #E8B887, dusty teal #3F6F8E, ink #2B2B2B. Around 3800K, gently muted with one warm accent.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-press paper, ink line, pigment pooling at edges, paper grain visible in the lights.
