# 1. Scene
One wide illustration set in Korea, divided exactly down the middle by a single vertical sepia ink line into two equal panels. The left panel is painted in faded sepia monochrome and the right panel in full warm colour. THEN: a 1980 Korean family in a small ondol room gathered around a tabletop radio on a low wooden table, father, mother and two children with anxious faces, a sealed letter and a folded blank newspaper on the table, a paper lattice window. TODAY: a Korean teenager in a hoodie and a Korean grandmother with white hair in a low bun sitting side by side at a bright kitchen table, comparing a smartphone with a blank screen, a tablet with a blank screen and a printed blank newspaper, a magnifying glass on the table, checking a story together. Each panel is a complete small scene with its own ground and sky, nothing crossing the centre line. Hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration with fine sepia ink line work, visible cold-press paper grain, soft bleeding pigment edges, matte finish, the handwriting of an illustrated memoir picture book. Every newspaper page, calendar sheet, poster, banner, sign and screen is a plain blank surface. All faces are invented fictional Korean faces, everyone is unharmed and unarmed, and every crowd is peaceful.

Eye-level wide shot, both panels the same scale and horizon height, the dividing line at the exact horizontal centre.
# 3. Lighting
Soft daylight from the upper left in both panels; the left panel muted and faded, the right panel clear and bright.
